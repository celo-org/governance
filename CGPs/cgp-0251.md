Proposal Description
============================

## TL;DR

Since August 2021 the Communities Guild has kept Celo's Discord, Telegram, Reddit, and X spaces safe, active, and coordinated with regional HUBs. Season 2 (Jan to Jun 2026) delivered against every KPI while operating fully autonomously.

Season 3 covers July to December 2026 with the same 4-person team and the same scope, at a leaner budget: 37,600 USDm + 10,000 CELO gross, minus 2,841 USDm and 53,569 CELO carried over from Season 2. That is a 41% reduction versus the Season 2 request.

The ask, denominated in CELO because the Community Fund holds CELO: 551,000 CELO. Priced at the lower of current spot ($0.0643) and 90-day average ($0.074), plus a 10% volatility buffer. Anything unused at year-end returns to the Community Fund or reduces Season 4.

Multisig (2/3, unchanged): `0x0B6a6666027d90fD56f4a2E1827296db4d590f4d`.

## Retrospective, Season 2 (Jan to Jun 2026)

**Moderation.** 24/7 coverage kept across Discord, Telegram (main plus regional and language channels), and Reddit. Code of Conduct enforced consistently, spam and scam attempts handled without escalations.

**Community programs.** Bi-weekly Community Hangout calls on Discord. Ongoing Guild.xyz quest campaigns and Community Steward rewards. X presence via [@CeloCommunities](https://x.com/CeloCommunities).

**Ecosystem coordination.** Regular collaboration with the Celo Foundation, cLabs, CeloPG, and the Celo Regional Council to keep information flowing between global and regional teams.

**Transparency.** Monthly reports published in the [Communities Guild reports thread](https://forum.celo.org/t/celo-communities-guild-reports/). Every spend logged in the [Detailed Spending Records](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1SEOnmhHipJ2WUQiANgzHTSsQrgR7TsWRlHcB_xsa820/edit) spreadsheet, reconcilable against the Safe's on-chain history.

**Autonomy.** Second full season operating self-managed, confirming the model established in H1 2025.

Same as prior seasons, this number may be refreshed to the executable rate at the moment the CGP goes on-chain. Anything realised above the gross budget returns to the Community Fund.

Risks
============================

- **Market risk.** CELO price movement between approval and conversion changes the effective USDm value of the ask. Mitigated by the 10% volatility buffer and by converting to USDm on arrival rather than holding.
- **Operational risk.** The 2-of-3 Multisig threshold and the published spending spreadsheet reduce single-signer risk and keep spend auditable.
- **Governance precedent.** The on-chain request is denominated in CELO because the Community Fund holds CELO, matching the direct-allowance pattern used in CGP-165, CGP-195, and CGP-219.
- No protocol, consensus, PoS, or protocol-economics changes are introduced by this proposal.
