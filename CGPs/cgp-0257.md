# Proposal Description

This CGP asks Celo Governance to allocate 25% of future Net L2 Sequencer Revenue to a permanent CELO burn and the remaining 75% to the Celo Community Fund. It preserves the existing revenue calculation and cost deductions under the revenue-distribution framework referred to as CGP-287 in the forum discussion.

Stablecoin-denominated transaction fees are already converted into CELO before distribution. This proposal adds a recurring supply-reduction mechanism while retaining most net revenue for ecosystem development. The burn scales with realized revenue; it is not a fixed treasury expense and does not guarantee any CELO price outcome.

The final forum proposal was posted on September 26, 2026, following community discussion, Governance Call #96 and technical discussions with Katy Gielty. ttghi presented it at Governance Call #97 on October 15, 2026. The Governance team subsequently requested this repository submission.

The forum's FINAL designation refers to the completed discussion draft. Repository status remains DRAFT until the proposal is submitted on-chain.

# Proposed Changes

## Allocation and scope

For each eligible bi-weekly distribution:

**Net Sequencer Revenue = Gross Sequencer Revenue − applicable operating/data-availability costs − OP Superchain revenue share**, using the existing approved accounting methodology.

Allocate that net amount as follows:

| Allocation | Percentage |
| --- | ---: |
| Permanent CELO burn | 25% |
| Celo Community Fund | 75% |

The calculation base is the net amount that would otherwise have been distributed in full to the Community Fund, before applying this split. Existing cost obligations and stablecoin-to-CELO conversion remain unchanged.

The policy applies prospectively to new Net L2 Sequencer Revenue. Existing Community Fund balances, previously approved funding commitments, existing allowances and the Governance Reserve are outside its scope. Revenue-backed staking is outside this CGP and may be considered separately.

## Transaction Descriptions

### Governance vote payload

This is a policy authorization to be implemented through the existing Operations Safe. The proposed governance payload contains no direct contract calls or treasury transfers. Approval and execution of the governance proposal do not themselves execute a burn or automatically reconfigure the Safe.

The empty-payload approach follows the repository precedent in [CGP-0254](cgp-0254.md), where a governance decision is subsequently implemented by a separate multisig. Governance Guardians should confirm that this approach is appropriate for this proposal before on-chain submission.

### Subsequent recurring Operations Safe transactions

The final forum proposal identifies the existing 2-of-5 Operations Safe as:

`0x7A1E98FC9a008107DbD1f430a05Ace8cf6f3FE19`

For each eligible distribution, the existing operators prepare one atomic, reverting Safe batch containing:

1. **Burn 25% of Net Sequencer Revenue.**
   - Destination: CELO token contract, `0x471EcE3750Da237f93B8E339c536989b8978a438`.
   - Data: call the existing `burn` method with the calculated burn amount, using the deployed contract ABI.
   - Native transaction value: zero, subject to confirmation against the deployed ABI during operational review; the burn amount is denominated in CELO base units and supplied to the method.
2. **Transfer the remainder to the Celo Community Fund.**
   - Destination: the same Community Fund recipient used by the existing approved revenue-distribution process.
   - Data: transfer the net amount minus the burn amount, using the established distribution method.
   - Amount: the Community Fund allocation calculated below.

These are recurring Safe actions, not transactions included in the governance payload. The existing signers continue to approve the batches. No new contract, multisig or signer set is proposed. The architecture does not introduce a new smart contract requiring an audit; the changed tooling and batch encoding still require technical review and simulation.

The final forum proposal describes the token's burn mechanism as crediting the canonical dead address `0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD`, with cumulative burns exposed by `getBurnedAmount()`. Operators must use the token's existing burn method, rather than substitute a plain transfer to that address.

Before activation, operators should confirm the Safe address, current threshold and owners, the deployed token ABI and burn semantics, the established Community Fund recipient and the reverting batch configuration. A Safe batch must revert as a whole if either action fails; grouping two calls alone is not sufficient evidence of atomic execution.

## Precision

Calculate using integer CELO base units, with 18 decimals:

```text
netWei = eligible net revenue for the period
burnWei = floor(netWei / 4)
communityFundWei = netWei - burnWei
```

The burn never exceeds 25%. Any indivisible base-unit remainder stays with the Community Fund allocation. Do not use floating-point arithmetic or rounded whole-token report figures to prepare a batch.

This is an intentionally empty policy-vote payload, not a template for the recurring Safe batch. The period-specific Safe calldata must be generated from actual reconciled net revenue by the existing operators and reviewed before signing. No guessed recipient address, fixed historical burn amount or unreviewed Safe transaction is included.

## Start and failure handling

Start with the first full bi-weekly revenue period after governance approval and operational implementation. Publish the exact first eligible period before activation to avoid applying the policy to a period already in progress.

If a batch reverts, neither allocation should execute. Operators correct the issue and resubmit. If an eligible distribution is missed, carry forward the unprocessed revenue and its corresponding burn obligation into the next distribution, with period-by-period reconciliation to avoid double counting or duplicate burns.

## Reporting

Extend each bi-weekly report to include:

- Gross revenue, stablecoin fees and CELO acquired through conversion.
- Operating/data-availability costs and Superchain revenue share.
- Net revenue before the split.
- Burn allocation and Community Fund allocation.
- Safe batch transaction hash and references identifying both actions within that batch.
- Cumulative CELO burned under this policy, separately identified from all historical burns.
- Any delays, failed batches, retries and carried-forward amounts.

Use the token's `getBurnedAmount()` for aggregate burn verification, and reconcile the policy-specific total against the individual batches: the aggregate counter may include burns from other sources.

For illustration only, applying the policy to the September 1–15, 2026 net revenue of 673,236 CELO would have burned 168,309 CELO and allocated 504,927 CELO to the Community Fund. This historical example creates no retroactive burn obligation.

## Governance control and review

The 25% rate remains in force unless changed by a future Celo Governance vote. Operational contributors and Safe signers are not authorized by this policy to choose a different percentage independently. The policy is implemented operationally; this proposal does not install an on-chain percentage parameter or technically remove the Safe signers' existing discretion.

Conduct an initial review approximately six months after implementation. Review revenue, costs, stablecoin conversion, burns, Community Fund inflows and expenditure, supply dynamics, network/payment/FX activity where measurable, distribution reliability and reporting quality. The review does not automatically pause, terminate or change the allocation.

# Verification

Before on-chain submission:

1. Confirm the CGP number and filename with repository maintainers; 257 is provisional based on the repository snapshot used to prepare this draft. Keep the governance proposal ID and execution date blank until known.
2. Confirm the empty policy-vote payload with Governance Guardians and confirm operational implementation with the existing Safe operators.
3. Validate the Markdown/front matter with the repository validator and parse `mainnet.json` as JSON; it must contain no governance calls.

Before activation:

1. Publish the first eligible revenue period, operator-reviewed tooling revision, and confirmed Community Fund recipient.
2. Verify the Safe's current owners/threshold and deployed CELO contract ABI at a recorded block.
3. Simulate a representative funded Safe batch. Verify the burn amount, the token's burn-accounting increment and the Community Fund's receipt of the remainder.
4. Exercise both failure paths in simulation: a failing burn and a failing transfer must each revert the complete batch.
5. Verify precision for net amounts of 0, 1, 3, 4 and 5 base units and for normal distribution amounts. Check that burn plus Community Fund allocation equals net revenue and that duplicate processing is prevented.

After activation, reconcile actual revenue, both actions in every batch and the reported cumulative policy burn. Successful execution of the empty governance payload is evidence of the governance decision, not evidence that the recurring burn tooling has been activated.

These operational simulations are acceptance criteria; this PR does not claim they have already been completed.

# Risks

- **Opportunity cost:** burned CELO cannot fund ecosystem initiatives; 75% of future net revenue remains available to the Community Fund.
- **Irreversibility:** completed burns cannot be reversed; changes to future allocations require governance approval.
- **Revenue volatility:** the absolute burn amount will vary and may be economically modest.
- **Operational dependency:** implementation depends on the existing Safe operators and tooling; the vote alone does not enforce future compliance. Public reconciliation makes missed or incorrect allocations visible.
- **Accounting and encoding errors:** wrong amounts, recipients, duplicate periods or a non-atomic batch could cause loss or policy deviations. Integer arithmetic, operator review and simulation are required before activation.
